Tell Kandoo once. It remembers when it matters.

Most assistants wait to be asked. Kandoo listens to how you actually talk, turns it into memories and actions, and brings each one back at the right time, the right place, or with the right person. Our two-minute video can't show all of it, so here is everything Kandoo does today, with things you can say and what happens next.

One loop, everywhere in the app

  1. CaptureSpeak, type, photograph a page, or talk to Mr. Kandoo.
  2. UnderstandOne utterance becomes many actions: facts, reminders, questions.
  3. LinkEach is tied to who, where and when.
  4. SurfaceIt returns at a time, on arrival somewhere, or when a person comes up.

What makes it different

No structure needed

You never fill in a form or design a page. A 90-second ramble comes out as clean, separate memories and reminders with real times.

It knows where you are, without tracking you

Reminders fire as you walk into a place you drew. Only those places are watched, and your location never leaves the phone.

Nothing saves without your yes

Every action arrives as a card you can check and edit. The AI proposes; you decide.

A colleague you can talk to

Mr. Kandoo holds a real conversation and acts across the whole app: reminders, notes, research, places, your team.

Capture

  • Speak with a live transcript, pause and resume, or type
  • Photograph or pick a photo, with an optional caption
  • One utterance becomes many actions: memories, reminders, questions
  • Relative times ("before 5", "tomorrow morning") resolved on your phone's clock

Review before anything saves

  • Every extraction arrives as a card you can edit
  • Save, change or dismiss each action
  • Colour shows state: listening, needs you, saved

Reminders

  • Local notifications that fire with no internet
  • Alarms that show over the lock screen
  • Repeat on chosen weekdays, snooze, complete with undo
  • Grouped into Today, Overdue, When you arrive, Upcoming
  • On arrival or on leaving a place Personal

Memory and recall

  • Ask in plain words; meaning-based search, not keyword matching
  • Answers draw on memories, reminders, notes, your Library and team files
  • Spoken answers in the phone's voice, or Kandoo's own Personal
  • Free answers from the last 10 days; your whole history Personal

People and photos

Places Personal

Try it

One sentence, four actions

Free

"Just came out of the meeting with Jed. He's pushing the API migration to Q1 because of the vendor issue, and the budget got cut by fifteen percent. I need to send Michael the spec before 5, and remind me to book the review room tomorrow morning."

  • Two memories, each a standalone fact: the migration moved to Q1, the budget cut by 15%.
  • Two reminders: "Send Michael the spec" today at 5:00 PM, "Book the review room" tomorrow at 9:00 AM.
  • Shown as a review card. Nothing is scheduled until you tap Save; then they fire on the phone, even offline.

Point the camera at the world

Free

Photograph an event flyer, a business card or a whiteboard.

  • Kandoo reads it and pulls out what matters: the event, date, place and people.
  • Later, "When is the outreach?" gets the answer from the photo you took.

Reminders that wait for a place

Personal

"Remind me to call my mother when I get to the hostel."

  • A reminder tied to the hostel you drew on the map, with no time attached.
  • It fires the moment you walk in. Your whole history stays answerable, and Insights charts how you used Kandoo this week or month.

Paper to your whole team

Pro

Photograph a page of handwritten lecture or meeting notes.

  • It becomes a clean, organised note, filed into the right Library category ("Kandoo Project", "Biology Notes").
  • Share it to your team in one tap. Everyone sees who sent it and when.
  • Open it in Microsoft Word to keep editing, and search inside every PDF and document your team shares.

Research with real sources

Elite

"Mr. Kandoo, research how student startups should price a subscription app, and write it up for my Kandoo Project."

  • He tells you the findings out loud, saying only what the sources support.
  • A card holds the write-up with numbered references and links. Say "yes" and it's in your Library.

Run your team by talking

Elite

"Summarise the PDF Ama shared, then tell the team to send their slides by Friday at noon."

  • He reads the shared file and gives you the key points.
  • A team-task card for Friday at 12:00 PM, to everyone. Say "yes" and it's sent.
  • Each teammate accepts it into their own reminders, and you see who accepted and who's done.

Ask about your own week

Elite

"How did I use Kandoo this week?"

  • "You captured 46 things and set 24 reminders. Your busiest day was Monday the 28th. Want me to open your charts?"
